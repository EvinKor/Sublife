# SubLife Membership Tier Benefits

## Tier Overview

### 🥉 Bronze Tier
- **Monthly Fee**: RM 29.90
- **Points Multiplier**: 1x
- **Benefits**:
  - Access to basic wellness content
  - Monthly health newsletter
  - 5% discount on partner pharmacies
  - Basic telemedicine consultation (2/month)

### 🥈 Silver Tier
- **Monthly Fee**: RM 59.90
- **Points Multiplier**: 1.5x
- **Benefits**:
  - Everything in Bronze, plus:
  - Priority customer support
  - 10% discount on partner pharmacies
  - Unlimited telemedicine consultations
  - Access to wellness workshops (2/month)
  - Annual health screening voucher (RM 200)

### 🥇 Gold Tier
- **Monthly Fee**: RM 99.90
- **Points Multiplier**: 2x
- **Benefits**:
  - Everything in Silver, plus:
  - Dedicated account manager
  - 15% discount on partner pharmacies & clinics
  - Family plan add-on (up to 3 members at 50% off)
  - Quarterly health screening voucher (RM 500)
  - Priority hospital admission assistance
  - Complimentary annual dental checkup

### 💎 Platinum Tier
- **Monthly Fee**: RM 199.90
- **Points Multiplier**: 3x
- **Benefits**:
  - Everything in Gold, plus:
  - 24/7 concierge health support
  - 20% discount on all partner services
  - Family plan add-on (up to 5 members at 50% off)
  - Monthly health screening voucher (RM 800)
  - International medical referral service
  - Executive health retreat (annual, valued at RM 3,000)
  - Priority access to specialist appointments

## Upgrade Policy
- Members can upgrade at any time; the prorated difference is charged immediately.
- Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.
- Points earned at a higher tier are retained after downgrade.
